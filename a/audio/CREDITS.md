How the files were made from the sources: trimmed to the steadiest stretch (loops) or to the sound itself (one-shots), mixed to mono for effects, matched in loudness, and encoded as MP3 (64 kb/s effects, 128 kb/s music). The effects are embedded in `index.html` by `tools/embed_audio.py`; the music stays as files.

The short synthesised sounds (clicks, the quiz tones, the rewind, the whistle option) are made in `index.html` and have no source.
